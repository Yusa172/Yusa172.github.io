About

Final-year Economics student at the University of Beira Interior. Since September 2026 I have been interning part-time at a consultancy in Leiria that prepares funding applications for small companies.

There I build the financial models, check whether each company meets the eligibility rules and write parts of the applications.

I work mostly with Excel, Power BI, R and Python. Outside work I build small analytical projects: a study of price volatility in the CS2 skins market, a Value-at-Risk calculator in Python and a study clock for a mini monitor.

Scouting and volunteering taught me to work in a team and to be someone people can count on.

Economics lens

I like projects where a dataset becomes a decision: prices, incentives, risk, and behavior explained with clean evidence.
